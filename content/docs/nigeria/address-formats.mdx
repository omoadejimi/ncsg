## Start with the task

A delivery, an identity check and a service-area check may need different address details. Explain why you need an address. Confirm requirements with the service team before making a field mandatory.

## Choose fields that fit the address

- **Address:** allow free text for a house, building, street, compound, village or community. Do not require a street number where there is none.
- **Nearest landmark:** offer this when it helps someone find the location. Explain whether it is optional.
- **City or town:** keep this separate from the state or territory.
- **Local Government Area (LGA):** ask only if the service needs it. Explain the abbreviation and provide a way to correct an incorrect selection.
- **State or territory:** include the 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT).
- **Postcode:** explain why it is needed and which format the receiving service accepts.

Do not treat Abuja and the FCT as interchangeable fields. For an address in Gwagwalada, for example, the town can be Gwagwalada and the state or territory can be Federal Capital Territory.

## Help people enter a postcode

If a postcode is required, help people find or enter the correct one. Offer a manual entry route alongside lookup. Do not infer a precise postcode from an LGA or insert a made-up value to satisfy a partner system.

NIPOST provides a [postcode lookup service](https://www.postcode.gov.ng/). Confirm the format your delivery or verification partner supports before changing validation rules. A postcode is not a substitute for all other address details.

## Confirm the address

Show the entered address before submission and let people edit it. Preserve meaningful spelling and local detail. Accept common abbreviations; ask for confirmation before changing anything that could alter the location.

Test with people using non-street addresses and with the staff who deliver or verify the service.

## Evidence and limits

**Editorial guidance with an official lookup source.** The NIPOST link provides postcode lookup, not proof that every receiving system supports the same format. Validate delivery and verification requirements with the relevant partner and users. For contributions, use the [evidence record in Editorial standards](/docs/operations/editorial-standards).
