## Use these terms as a starting point

The wording below is editorial guidance. It is not evidence that a term has been tested with every Nigerian audience. Validate important wording with the people using your service.

All financial examples are illustrative. Replace bracketed placeholders with verified product information. Obtain product, operations and compliance review before using limits, fees, rates or promises in a live service.

## Fees

- Prefer **fees** in interfaces. Keep the wording consistent with the statement or agreement people will receive.
- Show each fee and the total payable before confirmation. See [Currency](/docs/mechanics/currency).
- Name the reason for the fee: “transfer fee” or “SMS alert fee”, rather than “miscellaneous charges”.

## Identity checks

- Spell out **Bank Verification Number (BVN)** and **National Identification Number (NIN)** on first use in each journey.
- Use “verify your identity” instead of “complete your KYC”. Explain the check where you ask for the information.
- Describe the information your service actually receives and how it uses it. Do not promise that an ID check cannot reveal account information without checking the integration and privacy notice.
- Explain account benefits and restrictions together: “Verify your identity to send up to [verified daily limit]. [Other conditions].” Do not present a limit as a universal regulatory rule.

## Transfers and recipients

Use [Nigerian English terminology](/docs/nigeria/nigerian-english) consistently. Distinguish a transfer that succeeded, a confirmed failure and a result that is still unknown. Only promise a return date when operations can support it for that transaction.

## Savings and loans

- Explain whether a rate is fixed or variable, the period it covers, how interest is calculated, when it is paid, and relevant fees or tax.
- If you show an interest amount, state the balance and assumptions. For illustration, 12% simple annual interest on a constant ₦100,000 balance is ₦12,000 over a year before fees and tax. This arithmetic does not promise monthly payments or describe a particular product.
- Show the amount received, instalments, total repayment, due dates and all applicable fees. Confirm that the figures reconcile.
- Name late fees directly and explain when they apply.
