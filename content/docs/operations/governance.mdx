## Roles

- **Contributors** — anyone who opens an issue, proposal or pull request.
- **Editors** — maintainers who review PRs, steward the [editorial standards](/docs/operations/editorial-standards), and decide proposals. Editors are listed publicly in the repository.
- **Working groups** — temporary groups for large efforts (e.g. a Hausa glossary), open to any contributor.

## How decisions are made

Decisions happen in public, in writing, on GitHub. The hierarchy is simple:

1. Evidence beats opinion.
2. Consensus among editors beats a single editor.
3. Where consensus fails, a documented vote of editors decides, and the dissent is recorded.

No organisation — sponsor, employer or government — gets editorial control. Funding and affiliations of editors are disclosed in the repository.

## Becoming an editor

Editors are invited from sustained, high-quality contribution: typically several merged contributions and constructive review activity over months. Any editor can nominate; existing editors confirm by consensus.

## Changes to governance

This page itself changes only through the [proposal process](/docs/operations/proposal-process), with an extended 30-day comment period.

## Content maintenance

The responsible role for each page is shown below its guidance. Until individual assignments are published, **NCSG maintainers** is the accountable role for accepting corrections and arranging review. This does not imply specialist approval of a page.

| Area | Responsible role | Backup | Review trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundations and accessibility | NCSG maintainers | Not yet assigned | Standards or user evidence change |
| Writing mechanics | NCSG maintainers | Not yet assigned | Format requirements or user evidence change |
| Interface patterns | NCSG maintainers | Not yet assigned | Implementation, service conditions or user evidence change |
| Nigerian terminology, identity and addresses | NCSG maintainers | Not yet assigned | Authority instructions, fees, partner formats or research change |
| Translation | NCSG maintainers | Not yet assigned | Source content, terminology or language review changes |
| Community and editorial processes | NCSG maintainers | Not yet assigned | Contact routes, responsibilities or the agreed process change |

The last-edited date records a source change. It is not a completed review. Record a specialist review only when its reviewer, scope, date, evidence and next review trigger can be linked publicly. Maintainers still need to name backups and arrange the required reviews.

Read the [changelog](/docs/operations/changelog) to identify changes and stable source commits. Use the [editorial evidence record](/docs/operations/editorial-standards#reusable-evidence-record) when submitting a review.
