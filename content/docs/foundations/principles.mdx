## Who this guide is for

This guide is for:
- content designers
- UX writers
- product designers
- developers
- researchers
- product managers
- teams building government and fintech digital services for people in Nigeria.


## The principles

### 1. Clear content helps people complete tasks

Writing in plain language is not about reducing information. It is about making information easier to understand and use.
If a sentence makes people feel excluded or unsure, review the sentence rather than blaming the user.

### 2. Precise words build trust

In financial services, unclear messages about money can cause confusion and concern.
Use accurate and specific language so people understand what is happening and what they need to do.

### 3. Nigerian English is a recognised standard

Terms such as airtime, BVN, POS and recharge appear in Nigerian digital services. Explain abbreviations and ambiguous terms where needed. Choose wording through audience research and use it consistently.

### 4. Design for different user situations

In your design, consider people using:
- low-cost Android phones
- limited mobile data
- devices in bright sunlight
- low battery levels
- unreliable network connections.

### 5. Use evidence to make decisions

Base your recommendations on:
- user research
- support requests
- usability testing
- accessibility standards.

Standards can establish requirements; research helps assess how wording works for a particular audience. State the source, context and limits. Label an untested editorial choice as a convention rather than a research finding.

### 6. Improve the guide together

Anyone can [propose a change](/docs/operations/proposal-process); decisions made and their reasons are also public.

## How to use this guide

- **Find guidance:** Search for a term to find related guidance. If you cannot find guidance for a term, suggest a new entry.
- **Suggest improvements:** If you think guidance on a term can be improved, please [Open a proposal](/docs/operations/proposal-process). Ensure you include your argument and supporting evidence.

## Evidence and limits

**Editorial principles.** These are NCSG’s starting points for content decisions, not a summary of a national user study. Record the audience, source and limits when applying them. For contributions, use the [evidence record in Editorial standards](/docs/operations/editorial-standards).
