## Names

Nigerian names come from hundreds of naming traditions. Never validate names against an English-centric pattern. Allow long names, short names, hyphens, diacritics, apostrophes and single names.

## Gender

- When gender is unknown, use "they".
- Ask for gender only when you genuinely need it, explain why, and include options beyond male/female where the regulatory context allows.

## Religion and culture

- Do not infer faith or holiday preferences from someone’s name or location. Use greetings appropriate to the context and audience.
- Avoid idioms tied to one culture when writing for all of Nigeria.


## Age and digital literacy

Avoid copy that assumes app fluency ("just toggle it in settings"). Spell out the path: "Go to Settings, then Notifications."

<DoDont>
<Do>
Your name

Use the name you want us to address you by.
</Do>
<Dont>
Enter an English first name and surname.
</Dont>
</DoDont>

For a regulated identity check, explain why the recorded name is required and provide help with mismatches. Keep a preferred name separate where the service supports it. See [Government IDs](/docs/nigeria/government-ids).

## Evidence and limits

**Editorial guidance; needs audience review.** These examples are not evidence of a single naming, cultural or language preference across Nigeria. Check identity requirements and test name entry with the people using the service. For contributions, use the [evidence record in Editorial standards](/docs/operations/editorial-standards).
