## Dates

Write dates as **day month year**, with the month spelt out: **14 March 2026**. In tight spaces, abbreviate the month: **14 Mar 2026**.

To reduce ambiguity when writing about dates, avoid all-numeric formats like 03/04/2026 in user-facing copy.

<DoDont>
<Do>
Your loan repayment is due on 4 March 2026.
</Do>
<Dont>
Your loan repayment is due on 03/04/2026.
</Dont>
</DoDont>


## Time

- Use the 12-hour clock with lowercase markers: **2:30pm**, **9am**.
- Nigeria has one time zone (WAT, UTC+1). State the zone where a deadline, travel context or mixed audience could make the time ambiguous.

## Date ranges

Use "to" rather than a dash in running text: **2 to 6 June 2026**. This makes the relationship explicit without depending on punctuation being spoken. Test the final wording and date control with assistive technology.

## Evidence and limits

**Editorial convention.** These are display defaults, not evidence that every Nigerian audience prefers the same format. Test date entry, deadlines and time-zone wording for the task. For contributions, use the [evidence record in Editorial standards](/docs/operations/editorial-standards).
