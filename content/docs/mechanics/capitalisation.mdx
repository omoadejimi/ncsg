## Default: sentence case

Use sentence case for headings, buttons, labels, menu items, and notifications. Capitalise only the first word of a sentence and proper nouns.

<DoDont>
<Do>
Send money to a new recipient
</Do>
<Dont>
Send Money To A New Recipient
</Dont>
</DoDont>

Sentence case is NCSG’s editorial convention for consistent interface text. Preserve proper names and recognised abbreviations.

## Proper nouns and product terms

- Capitalise real names. For example: **Lagos**, **First Bank**, **Ramadan**.
- Capitalise official scheme names: **Bank Verification Number (BVN)** on first use, **BVN** after.
- Don't capitalise generic features to make them sound important: it's "your wallet", not "your Wallet".

## Avoid all capitals for sentences

Avoid all capitals in sentences and instructions. Use layout and emphasis to help people scan. Keep established abbreviations such as BVN and NIN, explain them on first use, and test pronunciation where it matters.

## Evidence and limits

**Editorial convention.** Sentence case is NCSG’s chosen default. No NCSG comprehension or screen-reader study is linked to this choice. Check important abbreviations in the actual interface. For contributions, use the [evidence record in Editorial standards](/docs/operations/editorial-standards).
